# Domain model

## Routing

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Knowledge Center | Place or institution where relevant expertise or activity concentrates |
| Knowledge Language Share | Estimate of topic knowledge by language; a routing prior, never a quota |
| Expertise Lane | Language/community search route derived from concentrated expertise |
| Affected-Community Lane | Route for populations that use, repair, experience, adapt, or resist the subject |
| Comparison Lane | Optional bounded English route for international, diaspora, or comparative evidence |
| Community Catchment | Population whose perspectives a community substantially reflects, independent of hosting location |
| Perspective Scope | Local, diaspora, professional, or global provenance label |

Assign every lane a priority:

- **required**: strongest expertise routes and materially affected populations;
- **conditional**: activate on disagreement, transfer risk, or a missing
  perspective;
- **deferred**: plausible but outside current budgets; report it explicitly.

## Discovery and evidence

| Stage | Artifact |
|---|---|
| Domain-scoped web search | Candidate Discussion |
| Direct retrieval and assessment | Bilingual Evidence Card |
| One lane deep dive | Evidence Packet |
| Cross-lane composition | Claim Map and Evidence Set |
| Insufficient evidence | Coverage Gap |
| Retrieval unavailable | Access Gap |

Use Evidence Roles to make portfolios complementary: broad discovery, specialist
scrutiny, local experience, and ownership/failure reports are common roles, not a
fixed taxonomy.

Assess discussions with locally meaningful signals: firsthand specificity,
Experience Date, corroboration, disclosure, author history when appropriate,
substantive disagreement, and community correction. Upvotes and repetition measure
attention, not truth.

Perform Lane-Relative Assessment before cross-lane composition. Preserve strong
minority-language evidence instead of forcing every discussion into one global
rank.

## Search and stopping

Create a Lane Lexicon from translated seeds plus native scripts, spellings,
acronyms, slang, and topic-specific phrases. Add terms from early relevant results
only when their meaning and source remain traceable.

Set an Evidence Horizon from the topic. Use weeks or months for changing services
and product conditions; allow years for durable practice. Record when the
experience occurred separately from the post date.

Stop at Lane Saturation when bounded follow-up searches mostly repeat represented
claims, communities, and perspectives and important roles are covered or marked as
gaps. Otherwise stop at the explicit search or context budget.

Track Source Lineage. Treat an original account and its translations, quotations,
cross-posts, and syndication as one Evidence Family. Derivative comments may add
independent analysis, but the underlying story counts once.
